Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

StackMinds AI ("StackMinds," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, stored, and protected when you use the StackMinds AI mobile application and related services.

1. Information You Provide

StackMinds AI allows users to enter information such as thoughts, notes, tasks, goals, focus plans, mood information, wins, and other productivity-related content.

Information you voluntarily enter into the app may be used to provide the features you request, including organizing information, generating task suggestions, creating focus plans, and providing AI-assisted productivity features.

2. AI Processing

Certain StackMinds AI features may send text that you intentionally submit to our application servers and third-party artificial intelligence service providers in order to generate the requested response or organization.

This information is processed only as necessary to provide the requested feature, maintain the service, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

Users should avoid entering highly sensitive personal information that is not necessary to use the app.

3. Microphone and Speech Recognition

StackMinds AI may request permission to access your device microphone when you choose to use voice-input or speech-to-text features.

Microphone access is used only when you activate a feature that requires voice input. Speech recognition may be processed by your device, operating system, or the speech-recognition service available on your device.

StackMinds AI does not intentionally retain raw microphone recordings on its own servers unless a future feature clearly informs you otherwise and obtains any required permission.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Some StackMinds AI information may be stored locally on your device, including settings, task information, history, wins, mood information, focus preferences, and other app state.

Locally stored information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the application.

5. Technical Information

When you use StackMinds AI, limited technical information may be processed automatically in order to operate and secure the service. This may include information such as:

  • Device or browser type
  • Operating system information
  • App version
  • Error or diagnostic information
  • Network and server request information
  • Approximate technical information such as IP address

This information may be used for security, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, fraud prevention, and service reliability.

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide StackMinds AI features
  • Organize user-submitted information
  • Generate AI-assisted responses
  • Provide focus and productivity tools
  • Remember app preferences and history
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve application stability and security
  • Prevent abuse or misuse of the service

7. Data Sharing

StackMinds AI does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the application, such as hosting providers, infrastructure providers, speech-recognition providers, and artificial intelligence providers.

These providers receive information only as reasonably necessary to perform services used by StackMinds AI.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, StackMinds AI, or others.

8. Advertising

StackMinds AI does not currently use personal information for targeted advertising and does not sell personal data to advertisers.

9. Data Retention

Information stored locally remains on the user's device until it is removed by the user, the application's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Information processed through our servers or service providers may be retained only for the period reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, comply with legal obligations, and enforce applicable policies.

10. Data Deletion

Users may remove locally stored StackMinds AI information by using available deletion features, clearing application data, or uninstalling the app.

You may also contact us to request deletion of personal information associated with StackMinds AI where applicable.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security, and users should use appropriate care when submitting information online.

12. Children's Privacy

StackMinds AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. Third-Party Services

StackMinds AI may rely on third-party technology and infrastructure to provide certain functionality. Those services may process limited information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, legal requirements, or our data practices change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
